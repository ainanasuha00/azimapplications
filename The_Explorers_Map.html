Our learning goal

By the end of these activities, you should be able to name familiar places, understand simple directions and use direction words to guide someone.

ACTIVITY 1 OF 3

Discover, Learn & Listen

Let's explore our town and learn how to give directions. Complete the three small steps below.

How to complete this activity

  1. First, look carefully at the town map and find the places you know.
  2. Next, choose a building to learn its name and what people do there.
  3. Then, read and say the direction words aloud.
  4. Finally, listen to the directions and answer the questions.
STEP 1

Discover the Town

Tap a place on the map to discover what it is.

Choose a place to see its name and description. 😊
STEP 2

Learn the Direction Words

Look at each symbol. Say the expression aloud and remember what it means.

↑

Go straight

Keep moving forward.

←

Turn left

Change direction to your left.

→

Turn right

Change direction to your right.

↔

Cross the road

Move safely to the other side.

▣ ▣

Next to

Beside another place.

▣ ⇆ ▣

Opposite

Across from another place.

STEP 3

Listen & Navigate

Listen to the directions. You can play them again.

Listen carefully: Start at the school. Go straight. Turn left at the park. The hospital is on your right.

Press play to hear the directions.

1. Where does the journey start?

2. Which direction should you turn?

3. What is the final destination?

ACTIVITY 2 OF 3

Where Am I?

Let's play the Wordwall game! Read each clue and find the correct place in town.

How to play

  1. First, click the Start button in the game below.
  2. Next, read the clue carefully.
  3. Then, choose the place that matches the clue.
  4. Keep going until you finish the game. Do your best!

ACTIVITY 3 OF 3

Route Rescue: The Lost Tourist

A tourist is lost in our town! Become a helpful guide and help the tourist find the right places.

How to play

  1. First, read the mission carefully.
  2. Next, look at the map and the choices on the screen.
  3. Then, choose the correct route to help the tourist.
  4. Complete each challenge and try to rescue the tourist. Good luck, explorer!
